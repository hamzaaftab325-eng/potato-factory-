SMALL DETAILS / BIG FEELINGA STUDY IN SATISFACTION
01The choreography+

Conveyors, blades, seasoning drums and sealers share a single clock. The factory repeats in a continuous 16-second cycle, with every moving part built and animated in code.

02The material study+

Brushed steel, enamel, curved chips and softly folded packaging. Procedural textures and a studio lighting rig give the tiny factory its own tactile world.

03The soundscape+

Water, blade ticks, frying, tumbling and sealing are generated as separate sound layers. Enable sound to hear the process in focus, or add the original rhythmic music loop.
